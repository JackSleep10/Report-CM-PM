## Platform

web

## Users

Five operational roles across Thai Lion Air Ground Support Equipment (GSE) at Don Mueang (DMK):

- **Requester (Ramp / Ground Staff):** Reports broken GSE vehicles on the tarmac or ramp; accepts completed equipment back into service (Return to Service); may use desktop or smartphone.
- **Technician (Workshop & Ramp Floor):** Executes CM repairs and PM Task Card inspections (Sections B–F, checklists, consumables, defects, spare parts), frequently on smartphones in noisy/bright outdoor tarmac or hangar environments under intermittent 4G.
- **Supervisor (GSE Maintenance):** Verifies completed CM repairs and PM Task Cards before managerial sign-off; sends incomplete work back for rework; tracks Weekly PM OPEN follow-ups.
- **Manager (GSE Maintenance):** Performs final regulatory approval on CM work orders and PM Task Cards (marking PM jobs `COMPLETE`); monitors Weekly PM OPEN summaries.
- **Admin (MNT Coordinator):** Syncs and repairs monthly Work Order sheets (`บันทึกการเปิด W/O`), imports monthly PM schedules (`100–500+` rows from Excel), manages Employee User IDs and PM Task Card form templates, tracks closed-job Drive scanning (`YES`/`NO`), and monitors active users.

## Product Purpose

Digital Corrective Maintenance (CM) and Preventive Maintenance (PM) dispatch, execution, approval, and official A4 form-printing system for Thai Lion Air Ground Support Equipment (GSE). Replaces paper handoffs and manual spreadsheet lookups while preserving regulatory signatures and exact official A4 PDF layouts.

## Positioning

A field-tested aviation GSE maintenance operations console tailored to Thai Lion Air's bilingual Thai/English DMK ramp workflow: zero-latency cached table rendering, native LINE group dispatch, Excel batch-paste scheduling, and 300-DPI vector/overlay A4 task-card printing synced across dual Google Spreadsheets (`Requests` and `บันทึกการเปิด W/O`).

## Operating Context

- **Environment:** Airport ramp/tarmac (bright sunlight, gloved or busy hands, frequent switching to LINE/Camera apps on mobile) and workshop/office desktops.
- **Data Architecture:** Google Apps Script Web App (`HtmlService`) inside an `ALLOWALL` iframe sandbox communicating via `google.script.run` to `Requests`, `Users`, `GSE MASTER`, and monthly tabs in `บันทึกการเปิด W/O`.
- **Rituals & Workflows:**
  - CM Lifecycle: `กำลังซ่อม` → `รอ Supervisor ตรวจสอบ` → `รอ Manager อนุมัติ` → `รอผู้รับอุปกรณ์ลงนาม` → `เสร็จสิ้น (Return to Service)`
  - PM Lifecycle: `รอดำเนินการ` → `กำลังซ่อม` (rework) / `รอ Supervisor ตรวจสอบ` → `รอ Manager อนุมัติ` → `COMPLETE` → `เสร็จสิ้น` (`CLOSED` in W/O sheet)
  - Monday Weekly PM OPEN Follow-up: Automated Monday reminder for Admin/Supervisor/Manager separating Motorized vs. Towable (`BC`/`TB`/`PS`) equipment with 1-tap LINE copy/share and A4 assignment print.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **GAS Iframe Sandbox Payload Budget:** `Code.gs` `doGet()` inlines `index.html`, `Stylesheet.html`, `PmEmbeddedTemplates.html`, and `JavaScript.html`. Staying well below the historical sandbox buffer ceiling (~`986 KB` baseline) is critical to prevent `about:blank` white-screen crashes. Heavy print assets (`PdfTemplateImage.html`, `PmTemplateImagesData.html`, original Drive PDFs) are lazy-loaded on demand.
- **Calibrated Official A4 Print Surfaces (Off-Limits):** `#printPage` (CM A4 overlay), `#pmPrintPage` (PDF.js 300-DPI + Vector A4 PM overlay), and `#pmWeeklySummaryPrintPage` (Monday follow-up sheet) are pixel-calibrated to Thai Lion Air official forms and must not have coordinates or print layout altered.
- **Auto-Generated File Rule:** `CorrectiveRequestApp/PmEmbeddedTemplates.html` is generated by `make_embedded_templates.py` and must never be hand-edited.
- **Business & Deployment Rules:**
  - PM items must never appear inside the CM Requests table.
  - `PROJECT_LOG.md` §2.1 notes PM navbar access was Admin-only during initial pilot, while current live `v175` code grants `admin`, `technician`, `supervisor`, and `manager` access (preserve current code behavior until confirmed otherwise).
  - Deployments must always overwrite BOTH official deployment IDs (`-i AKfycbyx...` and `-i AKfycbyr...`) and sync to GitHub (`JackSleep10/Report-CM-PM`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Thai Lion Air Identity:** Primary Crimson `#E31837` anchored in the top navigation shell and official headers, paired with crisp high-contrast Aviation Slate neutrals and semantic operational status colors.
- **Typography:** `Sarabun` bilingual Thai/Latin operational font with tabular numerals on Work Order numbers, request IDs, dates, HR meters, and KPI counters.
- **Operational Tone:** Clear Thai operational labels with standard English aviation abbreviations (`GSE`, `W/O`, `RTS`, `HR METER`, `Check A/B/C`). No developer jargon or decorative purple/rainbow chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Outdoor Ramp Readability:** WCAG 2.1 AA contrast (`≥ 4.5:1` for normal text and placeholders, `≥ 3:1` for large text/icons) across all statuses, buttons, banners, and form inputs.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Minimum `44×44px` interactive controls on touch viewports (`≥ 24×24px` target compliance for table/checklist checkboxes) and `16px` input font floor to prevent mobile Safari auto-zoom.
- **Keyboard & Assistive Tech:** Full landmark structure (`<nav>`, `<main>`), single active `<h1>`, `role="dialog"` + `Escape` + focus restoration across all modals, `:focus-visible` indicators, `<label for>` associations, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
